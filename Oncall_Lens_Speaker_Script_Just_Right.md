## ⏰ **TIMING REMINDERS**

- **Total presentation: 12-15 minutes**
- **Demo is your centerpiece: 3-4 minutes**
- **Don't rush the metrics slide - let them sink in**
- **Q&A should be conversational and confident**
- **If running long, you can shorten slides 9-11 (business)**
- **If running short, expand on the demo or add more technical details**

---

## 🎬 **FINAL TIPS FOR DELIVERY**

### **Voice & Presence:**
- Speak 10% slower than feels natural
- Pause after key points
- Vary your tone - conversational for story, confident for metrics
- Make eye contact with different sections of the audience

### **Technical Smoothness:**
- Have backup screenshots ready
- Test your demo 3 times before presenting
- Have a "demo fails" backup plan
- Keep water nearby

### **Energy Management:**
- Start strong with the 3 AM story
- Build energy through the demo
- Peak energy on the metrics
- Inspirational close

### **Key Phrases to Emphasize:**
- "513% improvement"
- "Minutes instead of hours" 
- "Every hero's journey"
- "$25.5 billion market"
- "Transform from zero to hero"
